# Subdirectories

* [datasets](datasets/index.md) - BigQuery dataset namespace hosting the HP Dragonfly structured ontology tables and Property Graph.
* [tables](tables/index.md) - 8 structured BigQuery tables covering models, SKUs, official spare parts, disassembly dependencies, ports/docks, BIOS/NVM, diagnostic codes, and community troubleshooting cases.
* [references](references/index.md) - Curated engineering reference documents extracted from the HP Dragonfly Folio 13.5 G3 Maintenance and Service Guide (N17344-001).
* [metrics](metrics/index.md) - Standardized serviceability and hardware reliability metrics.
* [computations](computations/index.md) - Sanctioned BigQuery SQL and GQL Property Graph queries configured as OKF v0.2 Attested Computations.
* [playbooks](playbooks/index.md) - Actionable field service and AI agent triage playbooks linking LED blink codes to HP Sure Start recovery and FRU disassembly.
