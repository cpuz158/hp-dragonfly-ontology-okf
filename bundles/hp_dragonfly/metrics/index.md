# Concepts

* [component-service-complexity-score](component-service-complexity-score.md) - Number of prerequisite hardware components and fastener groups that must be removed before servicing a target FRU.
* [hardware-fru-replacement-rate](hardware-fru-replacement-rate.md) - Proportion of weighted community and field troubleshooting incidents that require physical spare-part (FRU) replacement rather than firmware/SoftPaq remediation.
