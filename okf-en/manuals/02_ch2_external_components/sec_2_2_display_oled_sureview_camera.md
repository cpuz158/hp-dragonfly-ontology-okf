# Chapter 2 Table 2-3 — Display Components: Low Blue Light Mode, Dual Microphones, 8MP IR Camera, Ambient Light Sensor & 6 Wireless Antennas (PDF p.13–14 · Doc p.7–8)

## 1. Display Overview and Low Blue Light Mode

The display assembly of the HP Dragonfly Folio 13.5 inch G3 2-in-1 Notebook PC integrates intelligent presence sensors, an advanced camera system, and integrated wireless antennas within its bezel and enclosure (PDF p.13, Doc p.7).

- **Low Blue Light Mode (Select Products Only)**:
  - The computer display is shipped from the factory in low blue light mode for enhanced eye comfort and safety.
  - Blue light mode automatically regulates blue light emissions when operating the computer at night or for extended reading sessions.
- **Safety & Comfort Warning**:
  - **WARNING!** To reduce the risk of serious injury, read the *Safety & Comfort Guide*. It covers proper workstation setup, posture, health habits, and critical electrical and mechanical safety guidelines (available at `http://www.hp.com/ergo`).

## 2. Display Components and Descriptions (Table 2-3)

The components housed within the display bezel and surrounding structural area are detailed below (PDF p.13–14, Doc p.7–8):

| Item | Component | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **(1)** | **User-proximity sensor** | Utilizes facial recognition to track user presence in front of the computer for energy efficiency and heightened security. When stepping away, the sensor shifts the device into the Sleep state. Upon returning, it recognizes the user and wakes the system automatically without requiring keyboard or mouse input. |
| **(2)** | **Infrared camera light** | **On**: Indicates that the infrared camera is actively in use. |
| **(3)** | **Infrared camera lens** | Enables the infrared functions of the camera (supporting Windows Hello biometric logon). |
| **(4)** | **Camera (8MP Resolution)** | Facilitates video conferencing, video recording, and still photography. Certain cameras support facial recognition Windows logon instead of password entry. (*NOTE: Features vary depending on installed camera hardware and software*). |
| **(5)** | **Camera light** | **On**: Indicates that the camera is active. |
| **(6)** | **Ambient light and color sensor** | Dynamically adjusts display panel brightness according to ambient lighting conditions and manages color temperature (warmth or coolness adjustment of the light source). |
| **(7)** | **WWAN antennas\*** | Transmit and receive wireless wide area network (WWAN) cellular data signals. |
| **(8)** | **WLAN antennas\*** | Transmit and receive wireless local area network (WLAN / Wi-Fi) signals. |

> **Sensor and Antenna Obstruction Notice (\*)**:
> The ambient light, color, and auxiliary sensors, antennas, and adjacent components may be externally visible. To maintain optimal wireless transmission efficiency, ensure the perimeter surrounding the antennas remains clear of obstructions. Regulatory notices are accessible via `HP Documentation` from the taskbar search box (PDF p.14, Doc p.8).

## 4. Posture Modes and Touchpad Configuration

- **Mode-Specific Keyboard Behavior (PDF p.14, Doc p.8)**:
  - In Stand, Tent, and Tablet operating modes, the physical keyboard (including function keys and the power key) is automatically disabled to prevent unintended input.
  - To restore keyboard and power key operation, reposition the convertible chassis into standard **Clamshell mode**.
- **Touchpad Setting Adjustments**:
  1. Select the **Search** icon in the Windows taskbar, type `touchpad settings` in the search box, and press Enter.
  2. Configure desired gestures, pointer behavior, or toggle the touchpad on/off.
