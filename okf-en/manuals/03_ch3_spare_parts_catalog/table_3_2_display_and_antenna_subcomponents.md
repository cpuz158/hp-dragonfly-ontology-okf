> **[Ontology & Service Engineering Linkage: Hinge Replacement & Disassembly Rules]**
> - For FHD configurations, subcomponent-level replacement is permitted using the FHD Hinge Kit (`N22935-001`) and the LCD Mid Frame (`N22923-001` / `N22924-001`).
> - For OLED configurations, hinge repair must strictly reference the dedicated OLED Hinge Kit (`N26147-001`) or verify full display top-assembly constraints due to fragile bonding layers.
> - When separating the panel from the mid frame or back cover, technicians must utilize stretch-release adhesive pull tabs (referencing adhesive part number `N09039-001` and related kits) by pulling horizontally and steadily to prevent mechanical panel fracture.
