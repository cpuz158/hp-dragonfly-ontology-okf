## 3. Engineering & Field Service Notes
- **OS Suffix Conventions**: Always verify the OS suffix when replacing system boards: `-001` designates non-Windows operating systems, while `-601` includes the embedded Windows operating system digital license.
- **Cable Kit Consolidation**: Subcomponent cables (touchpad, SIM board, transfer board, wireless charging module) are not sourced individually; they are provisioned under consolidated Cable Kit spare part number `N22929-001`.
