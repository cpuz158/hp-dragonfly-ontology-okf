# Chapter 5 §5.1–5.2 — Preparation for Disassembly & Bottom Cover (N22918-001) Removal Procedure (PDF p.39–41 · Doc p.33–35)

## 1. Overview and Preliminary Requirements (PDF p.39 / Doc p.33)

This chapter outlines the removal and replacement procedures for authorized service provider parts.

- **IMPORTANT Warning**: Only an authorized service provider should access the internal components described in this chapter. Unauthorized access can cause irreversible hardware damage or void the manufacturer warranty.
- **Service Tag Information (NOTE)**: Specific system details including computer model, serial number, product key, and warranty duration are recorded on the service tag located at the bottom of the computer.
- **Component Support Updates**: HP continually updates parts. Current supported part numbers can be verified via HP PartSurfer. Ensure each screw size and location is strictly recorded during removal.

### Preparation for Disassembly Steps
1. Turn off the computer. If uncertain whether the unit is off or in Hibernation mode, turn it on and perform a full operating system shutdown.
2. Disconnect AC power by unplugging the power cord from the computer.
3. Disconnect all external devices and peripherals connected to the computer.

## 3. Disassembly & Leather Cover Removal Procedure (PDF p.40–41 / Doc p.34–35)

### Releasing the Leather Wrap and End Plugs (PDF p.40 / Doc p.34)
1. **Display Positioning**: Open the display assembly to approximately **110°** to create sufficient slack where the leather cover wraps over the rear hinge spine.
2. **Removing Cover Plugs**: Insert a non-marring prying tool into the inside of the cover at both ends to release the cover plugs, then lift and remove both plugs.
3. **Removing End Screws**: Remove the **Phillips M2.0 × 4.0** screw positioned beneath each cover plug.

### Releasing the Leather Cover & Chassis Panel (PDF p.41 / Doc p.35)
4. **Disengaging Leather Cover**: The leather wrap extends around the rear spine onto the bottom assembly. Push the exterior edges of the leather cover at an angle inward from the outside to disengage the side retaining tabs, then push the center of the cover away from the chassis.
5. **Loosening Screws and Prying Cover**: Loosen the **five captive Torx T5 screws**. Insert a case utility tool into one of the designated pry slots along the bottom edge, then gently pry upward to release the retention clips and separate the bottom cover.

---

## 4. Reassembly Guidelines (PDF p.41 / Doc p.35)

- Reverse the removal procedures to reinstall the bottom cover.
- **Alignment Rule (NOTE)**: When replacing the bottom cover, toe the cover in along the bottom perimeter first, then snap it into place along the side edges. Confirm that all retention clips are firmly engaged before installing and tightening the side screws.
