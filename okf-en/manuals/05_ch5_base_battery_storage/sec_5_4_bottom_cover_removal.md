# Chapter 5 §5.4 — Bottom Cover (`N22918-001`) Removal Procedure (pp. 35–37)

## 1. Spare Part & Prerequisite Metadata
- **Spare Part Numbers:** `N22918-001` (Slate Blue) / `N22919-001` (Natural Silver)
- **Prerequisite DAG Position:** **Step 0 Root Prerequisite** — Required before accessing Battery, SSD, WWAN, Speakers, Fan, Heatsink, System Board, or Display Assembly.

## 2. Step-by-Step Disassembly Sequence (Service Guide p. 36)
1. **Position Notebook Upside Down:** Place the notebook top-cover down on a clean, ESD-grounded anti-static mat with the front edge facing you.
2. **Loosen 5 Captive Torx T5 Screws:**
   - Using a **Torx `T5` driver**, loosen the **five (5) captive `M2.0 × 4.5 mm` screws** securing the Bottom Cover (2 along the rear hinge edge, 3 along the front palmrest edge).
   - *Note:* These screws are retained by C-clips on the cover and do not come completely out; stop turning once you hear/feel the captive thread click.
3. **Release Retention Clips from Rear Hinge Seam:**
   - Insert a thin **non-magnetic plastic opening tool** into the seam between the Bottom Cover and Top Cover near the **left and right rear hinges**.
   - Gently pry upward along the rear edge and side perimeters until the internal magnesium retention clips disengage, then lift the Bottom Cover straight up and away from the chassis.
